# Scientific Manuscript Builder Suite for Claude

A collection of advanced Claude Skills designed to support the complete scientific writing workflow, from identifying research gaps to generating a submission-ready manuscript.

## Included Skills

### 1. Research Gap Finder
Identifies high-potential research questions through structured literature-gap analysis.

**Key features**
- Systematic research-gap discovery
- Topic novelty assessment
- PICO, PCC, SPIDER, PECO, PIRD framework selection
- Topic ranking and prioritization
- Publication feasibility assessment

---

### 2. Results Section Builder
Transforms extracted study data into a journal-ready Results section.

**Key features**
- PRISMA-compliant reporting
- Data extraction support
- Evidence synthesis
- Structured results writing
- Anti-hallucination safeguards

---

### 3. Discussion Section Builder
Creates an evidence-based Discussion section from Results and extracted literature.

**Key features**
- Agreement/disagreement analysis
- Literature contextualization
- Clinical and research implications
- Strengths and limitations analysis
- Future research recommendations

---

### 4. Manuscript Assembler
Integrates all manuscript components into a complete submission-ready paper.

**Key features**
- IMRAD structure assembly
- Cross-section consistency checks
- Citation reconciliation
- Reporting guideline compliance
- Journal submission preparation

---

## Core Principles

All skills follow the same standards:

- Evidence-first workflow
- No fabricated citations
- Verification before conclusion
- Transparent uncertainty reporting
- Journal-ready scientific writing
- Compatible with systematic reviews, scoping reviews, diagnostic reviews, and original research
